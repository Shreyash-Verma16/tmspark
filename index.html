03 — INTELLIGENCE LAYER

Two Engines. One Adaptive Writing Experience.

TypeMind Spark is designed around a dual-engine architecture that matches the complexity of each writing task to the right AI workflow—from instant everyday edits to context-aware, multi-step writing assistance.

🟣 AURA ENGINE Fast Responses · Everyday Writing

AURA ENGINE

Built for speed and everyday communication, AURA handles lightweight writing tasks with minimal friction, helping users express their thoughts clearly without disrupting their workflow.

  • Instant Writing Assistance — Quick replies, message suggestions, and everyday rewrites.
  • Clarity & Refinement — Grammar correction, punctuation, and sentence-level improvements.
  • Adaptive Tone Control — Adjust formality, phrasing, and tone to suit the conversation.
  • Default Processing Pipeline — Designed for frequent, low-complexity writing tasks.
Powered by lightweight AI models and optimized inference workflows. Default Pipeline
🔵 PRISM ENGINE Contextual Reasoning · Complex Writing

PRISM ENGINE

Designed for more demanding writing tasks, PRISM focuses on understanding broader context, interpreting communication intent, and generating responses that require more deliberate reasoning.

  • Contextual Rewrite & Synthesis — Transform rough drafts and complete unfinished thoughts while preserving intended meaning.
  • Context-Aware Personalization — Adapt suggestions to the audience, conversation context, and writing preferences.
  • Multi-Step Writing Workflows — Support complex rewrites, strategic communication, and longer-form composition.
  • Advanced Processing Pipeline — Reserved for tasks that benefit from deeper contextual analysis.
Designed for complex, context-intensive writing workflows. Advanced Pipeline

Legal Information

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Welcome to TypeMind Spark. TypeMind Spark is currently a technology prototype engineered to explore a context-aware and intent-driven way of interacting with AI through keyboard input.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you visit the TypeMind Spark website, test available prototype features, or join the development waitlist.

1. Information We Collect

We intentionally minimize data collection. When you visit the website, basic technical telemetry may be logged automatically (IP address, browser type, operating system, timestamp). This information is utilized solely for website maintenance, infrastructure health, and security defense.

2. Processing of AI Prompts and Queries

When you interact with interactive prototype modules, queries are routed through optimized LLM inference pipelines to generate real-time suggestions. Do not submit sensitive passwords, financial information, or confidential secrets into the prototype.

3. Storage and Data Retention

The TypeMind Spark prototype does not permanently store your testing session logs or build persistent external profiles. Temporary session state is terminated when your browser session concludes.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure

TypeMind Spark prototype hosting is provided via secure cloud infrastructure (Vercel). External inference is conducted across encrypted endpoints.
